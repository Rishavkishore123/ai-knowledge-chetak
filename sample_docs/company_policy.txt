
Company Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

4. Probation
New employees have a probation period of 6 months.

5. Leave Approval
Employees must submit annual leave requests at least 5 working days in advance.
Manager approval is required before leave is confirmed.
